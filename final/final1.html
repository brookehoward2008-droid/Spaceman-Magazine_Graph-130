Moon is now completely out of cheese

In what NASA officials are calling “a deeply preventable tragedy,” the first crewed mission of 2037 has returned with alarming news: the Moon is now completely out of cheese.

According to mission commander Lt. Zora “Snack Attack” Velasquez, the incident began innocently enough. “We were just taking core samples,” she explained, “and then Private Donnelly shouted, ‘Guys… this rock tastes AMAZING.’”

Within minutes, the crew had discovered that beneath the dusty lunar surface lay vast, delicious caverns of aged cosmic cheddar. Within hours, they had eaten it.
