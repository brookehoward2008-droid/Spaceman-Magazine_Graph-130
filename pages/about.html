About the project

Spaceman Magazine

A web translation of a 1960s space-age magazine layout, built for GRAPH 130 at Everett Community College.

Spaceman Magazine adapts the rhythm of a printed editorial spread to the browser: a bold hero, a twelve-column grid of stories, and article pages with tight reading columns.

The site is plain HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript — no frameworks. Layout is handled with CSS Grid, and the design simplifies gracefully on smaller screens.
